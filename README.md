Saudi financial documents are messy in a very specific way. A single filing can mix Arabic and English, RTL and LTR text, Arabic and Western numerals, irregular tables, scans, skew, and inconsistent layouts.

Arabic adds another challenge: characters like **ب / ت / ث**, **ج / ح / خ**, and **ر / ز** can differ by only a dot. Even Arabic numerals such as **٠ (zero)** and **٥** can become ambiguous under low resolution, compression, or poor cropping.

The real problem is not just OCR. It is whether a VLM can reliably understand **text, geometry, and reading order** across all this variability.
