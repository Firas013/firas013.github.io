Feras Aldawsari

Applied AI · Computer Science

Hi! I work where language models meet real, messy documents — mostly the regulated kind, where being confidently wrong is expensive. Most of that has been Arabic financial documents: audited filings, interim statements, and the arithmetic that has to survive them. I’m in Applied AI at Saudi Arabian Military Industries (Advanced Electronics), and interested in LLM systems, agentic architectures, evaluation harnesses, document intelligence, and inference optimization.

Some things I’ve built:

  • Equity Research Agent for 250+ Saudi-listed companies, combining deep filing analysis, margin/ratio computation, market context, and Zakat-aware financial interpretation across 1,024 Arabic/English reports (in production).
  • ArabicFinBench — an evaluation benchmark for Arabic financial-document understanding, scoring parse fidelity, extraction, and arithmetic consistency separately, because a statement that reads cleanly and does not add up is the failure mode that matters in finance.
  • Competed in the 2025 Drone-vs-Bird Challenge (ICASSP/AVSS) with a fine-tuned YOLOv11 pipeline trained on real and synthetic data, using preprocessing techniques including contour extraction and background subtraction.
  • now Currently exploring Recursive Language Models (RLMs) and context-folding harnesses for long-context and long-horizon reasoning. → Finance → Legal (against my better judgment)

Along the way I’ve had stints across fintech, defense AI, and medtech — usually somewhere between agentic RAG, OCR, and computer vision. The through-line is documents that carry real consequences: filings, statements, contracts.
